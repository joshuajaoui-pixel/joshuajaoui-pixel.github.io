RAGDOLL FOUNDRY

A physics test lab. Pick something from the catalogue, place it, then poke it. Every person has blood, bones, pain and consciousness; robots run on power and break into parts.

Q · E
Pick an item in the menu, hover the lab, then spawn it facing left (Q) or right (E). You can also drag an item out of the menu.
Hold left click
Pick up and throw.
A · D
Rotate what you are holding. Hold longer to spin faster, add Shift for faster still.
Alt + drag
Snap to a grid while dragging.
F
Activate: shoot, light fuses, start engines. With a weapon at someone's hand (easiest while paused), F makes them hold it.
C · V
While holding something, C copies it. V pastes at the cursor.
Backspace · Z
Delete what is under the cursor · remove the last thing spawned.
Right click
Menu: freeze, delete, copy, heal and more.
Arrows · middle mouse
Move the camera. Scroll wheel or numpad + / − zooms.
Space · G
Pause · slow motion. While paused you can drag and rotate anything freely.
S · T
Health view · temperature view.
Tab or C
Hide or show the interface.

Tools along the top: Rope, Spring, Hinge and Weld connect two points (click empty space on the second click to anchor to the wall). Freeze locks a part in place. Cut removes the connection nearest the cursor. Number keys 1 to 8 pick a tool.
